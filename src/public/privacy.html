Atithy Worker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

Atithy Worker helps workers discover work opportunities and complete onboarding through WhatsApp and the Atithy app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how workers can contact us.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information such as phone number and WhatsApp profile details.
  • Worker onboarding details such as name, language, gender, and work preferences.
  • Location details shared by the worker for matching jobs in the selected area.
  • Identity review documents or images submitted by the worker, where required for onboarding.
  • Messages, button clicks, and referral details from ads or WhatsApp conversations.
  • Technical logs needed to operate, secure, and debug the service.

How We Use Information

  • To register workers and support onboarding.
  • To communicate with workers through WhatsApp and the Atithy app.
  • To review worker submissions and help match workers with suitable opportunities.
  • To measure ad and onboarding performance.
  • To prevent abuse, protect accounts, and comply with applicable laws.

Sharing

We do not sell worker personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate WhatsApp messaging, hosting, storage, analytics, and worker onboarding. We may also share information when required by law or to protect workers, customers, Atithy, or the public.

Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect worker information. No online service is completely secure, so workers should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information unless requested for onboarding.

Choices and Requests

Workers can request access, correction, or deletion of their information by contacting us. Some information may be retained when needed for legal, safety, fraud-prevention, or operational reasons.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at atithi4us@gmail.com.
